Data Visualization & EDA

Exploratory analyses with a clear editorial point, including outlier detection in customer revenue, top products by revenue, and gender differences in IT.

Role

Analysis, visualization, storytelling

Method

IQR outlier detection, category comparison

Technology

Python, Pandas, Matplotlib

Status

Completed

Approach

Rather than pure exploration charts, every analysis starts with the editorial point fixed before the right visualization is chosen — clarity before technique.

Example: outlier analysis

  • IQR method (Q3 + 1.5 × IQR) to identify customers with unusually high spend
  • 151 customers (1.9% of the customer base) above the upper bound of €6,790
  • Outliers highlighted visually right in the histogram instead of in a separate table

Further analyses

Top 10 products by revenue with category highlighting (6 of the 10 bestsellers come from the Gifts category), customer age by age group, and gender differences in IT — each focused on one clear, self-contained core message per chart.
