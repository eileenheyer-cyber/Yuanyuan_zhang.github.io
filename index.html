About me

I have been living in Germany for seven years and come from a background in high-end fashion retail and e-commerce. Today, I’m strengthening my business understanding through my part-time distance studies in Digital Business Administration at IU International University of Applied Sciences. Along the way, I developed a stronger analytical mindset, especially in digital marketing and customer analysis, and became increasingly interested in how data can reveal customer behavior and help answer real business questions.

What I enjoy most is the full data journey, from raw data and modelling through analysis and visualization to building systems that make insights reliable. For me, it’s a bit like cooking, which I love: taking simple ingredients, understanding how they work together, and turning them into something meaningful. That’s the feeling I get when a dashboard comes together and helps a business see new opportunities for growth.

I’m naturally curious and enjoy figuring things out. When I come across something I don’t understand, I like to break it down, experiment with it, and eventually build it myself. That process of learning by doing is what keeps me interested and constantly pushes me to explore new areas.

I’m not interested in learning a tool just for the sake of knowing it. I want to understand how business, data, and technology connect — and use that understanding to build things that are genuinely useful.
